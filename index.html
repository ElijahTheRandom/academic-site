About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Texas A&M University; I joined Dr. Abdullah Muzahid's group in Fall 2026. Previously, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Alabama in Huntsville, where I was advised by Dr. Chaity Banerjee and Dr. Tathagata Mukherjee, specializing in remote sensing and computer vision development. It also sparked my interest in the internal structures of generative AI and multimodal learning. While my background includes developing autonomous systems and remote sensing, my current research investigates how we can engineer AI/ML (recently agentic LLMs) systems to be more interpretable, efficient, and reliable.

As frontier generative systems push the limits of memory, throughput, and energy, what a model represents internally and how it runs on hardware become inseparable problems — I am excited to contribute at that intersection.

Research Interests

  • Representation learning
